# Main-text figure captions (final, 2 Nov 2026)

Product numbers refer to Table 1. Files: `png/Figure1–4.png` (600 dpi), `pdf/Figure1–4.pdf`; all figures are 170 mm wide.

**Figure 1.** Identification of the energetic pathway (half-power method), Product 1. (a) Kinetic energy (KE) of the monthly-mean geostrophic velocity in January 2001 (grey scale) with the half-power points detected in the ACC band (purple); star: example longitude of (b); light blue: sea ice. (b) KE profile at 147.9°E in January 2001 (solid) and its 2001–2025 mean (dashed). The 200 cm² s⁻² threshold (dotted) and 25 % of the peak KE (dash-dotted) define the envelope (shaded) between its southern and northern limits φ_S and φ_N; the half-power latitude φ_hp divides the KE integrated over the envelope into two equal parts.

**Figure 2.** Drake Passage, Product 1. (a) EKE trend, 2001–2025 (least-squares trends of annual means on 0.25° cells; no field significance is computed for this map), with the mean SAF and PF (solid black). (b) Half-power occurrence, 2001–2025 (% of months per cell), with the 10 % occurrence contours of 2001–2015 (cyan) and 2016–2025 (black, dashed). The note gives the overlap of the areas enclosed by the two contours (intersection over union) and the 2016–2025 minus 2001–2015 change in mean latitude, over 70–55°W (bracket above the map), of the half-power latitude and of the fixed ADT contour through the mean half-power position (negative: southward); these changes are computed on the 62 retained circumpolar tracks in 70–55°W, whereas the occurrence map uses all half-power envelopes in the region. Maps show the 3000 m isobath (grey) and the Gould transect (green), dashed where seasonally ice-covered. (c) EKE trend between the mean SAF and PF in 2° longitude bins, 86–40°W, with 95 % intervals (shading); dashed lines mark the passage (68–58°W); dark fill: mean seafloor depth between the fronts at 1/8° resolution (right axis). (d) Trend of the surface cross-track velocity along the Gould transect in ADT bins (bands between two monthly-mean ADT streamlines), 2001–2025, with 95 % intervals (bars), plotted against the mean ADT at the bin centre; grey bands: ADT ranges of the mean PF and SAF zones. The bins follow monthly-mean ADT streamlines and are not equivalent to the synoptic streamwise coordinates of Gutierrez-Villanueva et al. (2023). Trends in (c, d) are calibrated Theil–Sen estimates; filled symbols: uncorrected p < 0.05, open: p ≥ 0.05.

**Figure 3.** Circumpolar changes, Product 1, 2001–2025. (a) EKE trend on 1° cells; white dots: cells passing a 10 % false-discovery-rate control. (b) Half-power occurrence (% of months per cell) with the 10 % occurrence contours of 2001–2015 (cyan) and 2016–2025 (black, dashed) and the overlap of their enclosed areas (intersection over union). Both maps show the 3000 m isobath (grey) and sea ice (light blue); (a) also shows the mean SAF and PF (solid black). Ticks on the rim of the maps mark the longitudes of the bathymetric features labelled in (d). (c) KE and EKE averaged over the ACC band: monthly values (thin), 12-month running means (thick) and Theil–Sen trends with 95 % intervals (shading; solid line: p < 0.05); trend values at the right. (d) EKE trend between the mean SAF and PF in 2° longitude bins with 95 % intervals (shading), and mean seafloor depth between the fronts (dark fill, right axis); filled symbols: uncorrected p < 0.05, open: p ≥ 0.05; light grey: longitude window of Fig. 2c. PAR: Pacific–Antarctic Ridge; DP: Drake Passage; NSR: North Scotia Ridge; SWIR: Southwest Indian Ridge; KP: Kerguelen Plateau; SEIR: Southeast Indian Ridge; MR: Macquarie Ridge. Flow is from left to right.

**Figure 4.** Wind stress, eddy energy and isopycnal slope. (a) Eastward wind-stress trend, 2001–2025 (Product 2). (b) Trend of the signed cross-stream slope of the σ₀ = 27.3 kg m⁻³ surface, 2004–2025, in % of the local mean per decade (Product 5), over the ACC band plus 100 km with ice-covered cells removed; the colour scale saturates at ±8 %. In (a, b) white dots mark cells passing a 10 % false-discovery-rate control; maps show the mean SAF and PF (solid black), the ACC band plus 100 km (grey, dashed), the 3000 m isobath and sea ice. (c) Wind stress, EKE and isopycnal slope averaged over the common cells of the ACC band plus 100 km, as anomalies in % of their 2004–2025 means (12-month running means), with Theil–Sen trends for 2004–2025 and their 95 % intervals (shading); solid lines: p < 0.05, dashed: p ≥ 0.05; trend values at the right. (d) Fractional-anomaly regression coefficients of annual KE, EKE and slope anomalies (2005–2025, trends retained) on the wind-stress anomaly of the previous year, with bootstrap 95 % intervals; filled bars: p < 0.05, open: p ≥ 0.05. For small anomalies the coefficient approximates d ln X / d ln τ but is not an equilibrium derivative. Grey lines: equilibrium reference values of eddy-saturation model studies, 1 (dashed; Meredith et al., 2012; Marshall et al., 2017) and 0.7 (dotted; Stewart et al., 2023), against ≈ 0 for the slope and transport (Böning et al., 2008). (e) Correlation of detrended annual KE, EKE and slope (2004–2025) with the wind stress 0–3 years earlier; filled markers: pointwise p < 0.05; grey: pointwise 95 % envelope from phase-randomised wind surrogates (EKE); legend p-values are family-wise over the four lags. EKE and KE in (c–e) are from Product 1.
