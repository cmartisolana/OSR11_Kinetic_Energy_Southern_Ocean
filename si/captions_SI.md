# Supporting Information figure captions (final, 2 Nov 2026)

Product numbers refer to Table 1 of the main text. Files: `png/FigureS1–S7.png` (600 dpi), `pdf/FigureS1–S7.pdf`. Width 170 mm, except Fig. S3 (83 mm). Unless stated, trends are calibrated Theil–Sen estimates with 95 % intervals; filled symbols: p < 0.05, open: p ≥ 0.05 (p is rounded to three decimals, so p = 0.050 is open).

**Figure S1.** All-satellite (Product 1) and two-satellite (Product 4) products, 2001–2025. (a) KE and EKE averaged over the ACC band, 12-month running means in % of the 2001–2025 mean; solid: Product 1, dashed: Product 4. (b) ACC-band trends of KE, EKE, its low-frequency and sub-monthly parts and the along-stream speed, in % of the mean per decade. (c) The same for KE, EKE and along-stream speed in the Pacific, Atlantic and Indian sectors. (d) EKE trends of earlier studies over their own periods (black: Hogg et al., 2015, Indian and Pacific sectors, 1993–2012, with their 90 % intervals; Martínez-Moreno et al., 2021, 35–65°S, 1993–2020, no interval given) and of Product 1 over 2001–2025 for the same regions (green, 95 % intervals), with the Atlantic box of Hogg et al. (2015) and the ACC band.

**Figure S2.** Drake Passage EKE by year and by period. (a) Annual EKE between the mean SAF and PF in 2° longitude bins, 86–40°W, as anomaly in % of the 2001–2025 mean of each bin (Product 1; the band and bins of Fig. 2c); dashed lines: the passage (68–58°W); horizontal line: division between 2001–2015 and 2016–2025. (b) The anomalies of (a) averaged over the bins upstream (86–68°W), in the passage (68–58°W) and downstream (58–40°W), with the 2001–2015 and 2016–2025 means (horizontal bars); labels: increase of the 2016–2025 mean EKE over the 2001–2015 mean of each region. (c) EKE trends, 2001–2025, in % per decade, in three fixed regional domains, Products 1 and 4: upstream (100–80°W, ACC band), Drake box (80–52°W, 63–54°S) and downstream (55–35°W, ACC band), all excluding cells with sea ice. (d) Difference between the 2016–2025 and 2001–2015 mean EKE in the same domains, in % of the 2001–2025 mean (no significance test).

**Figure S3.** Cross-track velocity trend along the LM Gould transect in passage coordinates (25 km bins of distance from the northern end): in situ ADCP velocity at 70–118 m, October 2005–April 2019 (orange, 95 % intervals as bars; Gutierrez-Villanueva et al., 2023), and altimetric surface geostrophic velocity (Product 1) over the same period (green, dashed) and over 2001–2025 (green, solid, 95 % interval shaded). Grey fill: 2001–2025 mean altimetric cross-track velocity (right axis); light blue: seasonally ice-covered part of the transect. Labels mark the mean positions of the SAF, PF and Southern ACC Front (SACCF).

**Figure S4.** In situ (orange; Gutierrez-Villanueva et al., 2023) and altimetric (green; Product 1) records along the LM Gould transect, October 2005–April 2019, altimetry sampled on the same months. (a) Mean cross-track velocity: ADCP at 70–118 m and surface geostrophic velocity (r = 0.98 between the mean profiles; r = 0.72 and rms difference 15 cm s⁻¹ over all transect bins). (b) Mean eastward transport per 25 km bin: in situ over 0–760 m (Sv, left) and altimetric surface transport per unit depth (10³ m² s⁻¹, right; axes scaled so that the two totals coincide; r = 0.91). (c) Annual EKE north of 58.5°S, in situ (0–780 m) and altimetric (surface), each in % of its 2005–2019 mean (r = 0.89 for annual means; r = 0.67 for individual transects). (d) Cross-track velocity trend per bin. (e) Transport trend per bin, in situ (left) and altimetric surface transport per unit depth (right). (f) EKE trend per ADT bin (bands between two monthly-mean ADT streamlines), in % per decade, against the mean ADT at the bin centre; grey bands: mean PF and SAF zones. In (d–f), intervals are bars for in situ and shading for altimetry. Dashed vertical lines in (a, b, d, e): mean PF and SAF; light blue: seasonally ice-covered part of the transect.

**Figure S5.** Sensitivity of the half-power analysis, Product 1. (a) Circumpolar trend of the half-power latitude, 2001–2025, for the baseline parameters and for each alternative (threshold in cm² s⁻²; relative threshold: 20 % of the maximum KE at each longitude; fraction: of the peak KE; minimum width of the envelope), in km per decade (positive northward). (b) Trends in 5° longitude bins (bin centre; bins with too few retained longitudes are omitted). (c) Percentage of synthetic realisations in which an imposed shift, growing linearly to the value on the x axis over 2001–2025, is detected, i.e. its trend lies outside the central 95 % of the trends of the no-shift realisations (dashed: 5 %). (d) Median recovered shift (dashed: 1:1).

**Figure S6.** Half-power analysis in Product 1 (all-satellite, black) and Product 4 (two-satellite, grey), 2001–2025: trends of (a) the half-power latitude (km per decade, positive northward) and (b) the KE at the envelope peak (% of the mean per decade), circumpolar and by sector.

**Figure S7.** Energy pathway versus height contour on the 900 retained tracks (892 longitudes), Product 1, 2001–2025. (a) Circumpolar-mean anomaly of the half-power latitude (purple) and of the latitude of the fixed ADT contour through the mean half-power position (grey), in km (positive northward). (b) ADT at the half-power point (blue) and ACC-band mean ADT (black, dashed; 12-month running mean), in cm. Thin lines: monthly; thick lines: 12-month running means; straight lines: Theil–Sen trends (solid: p < 0.05, dashed: p ≥ 0.05), with values, 95 % intervals and p at the right.
