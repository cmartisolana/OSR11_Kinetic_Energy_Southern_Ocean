## 1 Title, take-home, novelty
- Working title (author decides; give alternatives in cover note): "Surface kinetic energy trends of the Antarctic Circumpolar Current, 2001–2025: anchored in Drake Passage observations". No "eddy saturation" in title.
- Take-home: altimetry reproduces the interannual variability of the ship-measured velocity and EKE in Drake Passage (and of transport north of ~58.5°S, but not full-section transport); extended to 2001–2025 and around the ACC, surface eddies and mean flow strengthen together along a pathway with no detectable displacement, while surface transport proxies change several times less. Consistent with eddy saturation; not a test of it.
- Novelty = HI §2 four points (in situ anchoring at matched coverage; matched conservative update of MM21/Hogg; mean flow with eddies; pathway bound with stated detectability + along-stream test of the surface ingredients of TNG14). Never "first"; never the extra years as the novelty. Put the four points in the last Introduction paragraph.
