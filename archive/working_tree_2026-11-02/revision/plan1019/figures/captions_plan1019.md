**Figure P1 | Drake Passage: coverage of the altimetric transport proxy and comparison with the in situ transport over matching latitudes.**

(a) Mean in situ total cross-track transport in the upper 760 m per 25 km bin, from the LM Gould ADCP transects 2005–2019 (Gutierrez-Villanueva et al., 2023) (orange bars). Mean surface geostrophic velocity normal to the altimetric section, 2001–2025 (blue line); dots mark the 53 points that are ice-free in every month and form the altimetric proxy. Shading marks the part of the section where the ice fraction exceeds 0.10 in at least one month (south of 58.5°S).

(b) Annual means of the in situ transport north of 58.5°S (orange) and of the altimetric proxy (blue), in standard-deviation units, 2005–2019.

(c) As (b), for the in situ transport over the full section.

(d) In situ transport north (orange) and south (grey) of 58.5°S for each transect, against the altimetric proxy of the same month, in standard-deviation units.

Correlations (text): north part r = 0.44 for transects, 0.72 for annual means; full section r = 0.05 and 0.11; north vs south part r = −0.55.

**Figure P2 | Surface EKE trends compared with earlier altimetric estimates.**

(a) EKE trends in percent of the period mean per decade with 95 % confidence intervals, over 2001–2020 and 2001–2025, for the all-satellite (green) and two-satellite (grey) products. Regions:
- the three sector boxes of Hogg et al. (2015): Indian 40–150°E, 57–44°S; Pacific 150°E–72°W, 62–48°S; Atlantic 35°W–10°E, 56–46°S;
- the 35–65°S domain;
- the ACC band.

Filled symbols: p < 0.05 (calibrated trend test); open: p ≥ 0.05. Diamonds: published estimates, which differ in period, product version and method:
- Hogg et al. (2015): crossover along-track EKE from 1993, relative to the 1993–2001 mean, bars at the 90 % level;
- Martínez-Moreno et al. (2021): area-integrated EKE south of 35°S, 1993–2020, no interval given.

Hogg et al. give no relative trend for the Atlantic box.

(b) Difference between the products' relative trends (all-satellite minus two-satellite), with 95 % confidence intervals.

(c) EKE integrated over 35–65°S, in percent of its 2001–2025 mean: monthly (thin) and 12-month running mean (thick). Shading marks the years added to the period analysed by Martínez-Moreno et al. (2021).

Pixels ice-affected in any month are excluded in (a) and (b), but not in (c), which follows the Martínez-Moreno et al. procedure.

**Figure P3 (Supporting Information) | Half-power analysis at standing meanders: coverage and synthetic tests.**

(a) Percentage of longitudes in each meander window, selected beforehand from bathymetry and climatology, at which a single half-power branch can be followed continuously in at least 90 % of months. The dashed line marks the 90 % criterion; only the SE Indian Ridge passes.

(b–d) Synthetic tests at the SE Indian Ridge (40 realisations per scenario: climatological KE plus observed monthly anomalies resampled in 12-month blocks, with a linear change over 25 years). Panels show the recovered 25-year change in (b) mean half-power position, (c) meander amplitude and (d) sinuosity, at 150 km smoothing. Scenarios:
- no change;
- 25 km northward shift of the jet;
- 25 km change in meander amplitude (flexing);
- uniform 20 % intensification;
- 20 % intensification of the northern flank only.

Pink dashes mark the imposed change.
